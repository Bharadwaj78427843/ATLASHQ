## Research Standards

All AERR reports must follow the Research Intelligence pillar requirements (Constitution §16.9):

1. Define the question, source strategy, evidence quality, and date boundary before synthesizing
2. Primary sources and reproducible evidence outrank summaries
3. Conflicting sources are represented by quality, not counted as equivalent votes
4. Research ends in a decision or a clearer uncertainty — not an impressive bibliography
5. Sourced findings are separated from interpretation
6. Adopted findings are linked to the decisions that depend on them
